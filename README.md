## LinkedIn Profile Optimizer Skill

**What it does:**
- **Headline optimization** — suggests improvements with keyword analysis
- **Educational keyword extraction** — identifies skills/credentials to highlight
- **Engagement tactics** — recommends how to attract job opportunities based on your background
- **Gap analysis** — compares your profile against jobs you're targeting
- **Read-only by design** — only suggests, never modifies your profile

**Key safeguards built in:**
- 🔒 Cannot access your LinkedIn account
- 🔒 Cannot make automatic changes
- 🔒 Requires your explicit approval for each step
- 🔒 You manually implement everything
---
## How to Use It

**Once the skill is installed**, ask the model things like:
1. *"Analyze my LinkedIn profile. Here's my current headline, summary, and these 3 job postings I'm targeting. What should I improve?"*
2. *"What keywords should I add based on my education and my target roles?"*
3. *"Identify gaps between my profile and Marketing Manager positions."*

The model will provide:
- ✅ Specific headline suggestions
- ✅ Keywords to add (with placement guidance)
- ✅ Engagement tactics tailored to your resume
- ✅ Gap checklist with priority ranking
- ✅ Step-by-step implementation guide
